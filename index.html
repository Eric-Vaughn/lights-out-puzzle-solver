Lights Out Puzzle Solver

Info

Hello and welcome to Eric Vaughn's Lights Out puzzle solver!

This interactive application allows you to click anywhere on the puzzle grid to design and solve puzzles. It also has buttons and configurable settings to play around with!

Buttons and What They Do

Solve — Solves the puzzle using the Light Chase method
Randomize — Creates a random puzzle layout
Reset — Resets the puzzle grid to an off state
